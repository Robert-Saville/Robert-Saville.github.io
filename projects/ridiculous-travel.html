Ridiculous Travel Agency

Extraordinary vacations. Impossible destinations. Questionable travel decisions.

THE PROJECT

A travel agency for places you probably shouldn't visit.

Ridiculous Travel Agency is a fictional travel website built around an unusual idea: what if you could book a vacation almost anywhere, regardless of whether that destination should be reachable?

From ancient civilizations to distant planets and legendary lost cities, the concept combines familiar travel website conventions with playful writing and imaginative destinations. I designed the multi-page experience in Framer, exploring visual design, page layouts, navigation, and creative storytelling.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Familiar travel design, with a few questionable destinations.

The visual direction draws from recognizable travel websites, using familiar layouts and promotional elements to make impossible destinations feel surprisingly bookable.

DESIGN APPROACH

Making an unfamiliar idea feel like a familiar experience.

I approached the project as a multi-page website, using recognizable travel layouts and navigation patterns to make the fictional concept easy to explore.

Working in Framer allowed me to develop the page layouts, organize content, and establish a consistent visual direction across the experience. The fictional destinations and playful writing became part of the design itself, giving the website personality while retaining the structure of a familiar travel agency.

REFLECTION

An experiment in making the ridiculous feel real.

Ridiculous Travel Agency gave me an opportunity to explore a more playful approach to web design, combining familiar website layouts with an imaginative concept. Designing multiple pages helped me experiment with visual consistency, content organization, and how a website's personality can carry across an experience.

The result is a fictional website concept documented through its visual designs and page layouts. It is an exploration of web design and creative direction rather than a completed, production-ready booking service.
