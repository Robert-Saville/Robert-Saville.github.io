DoktorEdge

A collaborative e-commerce website design for DoktorEdge, a local blacksmith business. The project focused on creating a clear, cohesive interface that presents handcrafted products and services through a modern digital experience.

THE PROJECT

Designing a digital experience for a traditional craft.

DoktorEdge was a collaborative university project focused on designing an e-commerce website for a local blacksmith business. Our team developed the site's visual direction, page structure, and interface to present the business and its products in a clear, approachable way.

My work focused on the interface and its visual presentation. I contributed UI design, wireframes, page layouts, layout diagrams, and design documentation as part of a four-person team.

THE CHALLENGE

Bringing craftsmanship into a modern digital space.

The challenge was to translate a hands-on craft into an online shopping experience. The website needed to reflect the character of the blacksmith business while making its products, services, and information easy to explore.

Our team aimed for a visual style that felt distinctive without getting in the way of browsing. Clear page structure, consistent styling, and straightforward navigation helped bring the different parts of the site together.

The resulting direction brought together a restrained color palette, consistent typography, and repeatable layout choices to create a cohesive website.

WEBSITE WALKTHROUGH

Exploring the storefront.

The website brings together several product categories under one storefront, giving visitors a central place to explore the business and its offerings.

The main shopping experience is organized around product discovery, with navigation and category groupings helping visitors move between different types of handcrafted goods.

The storefront homepage introduces the business and provides an entry point into the shopping experience.

Browsing product categories.

The storefront organizes its products into distinct categories, including knives, wood and burl, and coffee. This structure gives visitors a straightforward way to browse different areas of the business without overwhelming the main navigation.

Product categories organize the storefront's different offerings into clear shopping sections.

Viewing individual products.

Individual product pages give visitors a closer look at an item before adding it to their cart. This part of the experience provides a more focused view of product information and purchasing options.

An individual product page showing the details presented during the shopping process.

CUSTOM KNIFE BUILDER

A more personalized shopping experience.

One of the more distinctive features of the website is its custom knife builder, which allows visitors to configure a knife through a series of selections.

Rather than presenting a single fixed product, the builder guides visitors through several stages of customization. These include choosing a material, knife type, size, and pattern.

As selections are made, the interface updates the build summary and price. This gives visitors a running view of their configuration before adding the finished selection to their cart.

Step 01 — Choose a material.

The first step allows visitors to choose the material for their custom knife. Different material options can affect the final price of the build.

The material selection step presents the available steel options and associated pricing.

Step 02 — Select the knife type, size, and pattern.

The following stages let visitors refine the configuration by choosing the type of knife, its size, and a pattern. Breaking the process into individual steps keeps the customization experience organized and easy to follow.

The remaining configuration steps allow visitors to personalize the knife.

Reviewing the finished configuration.

Once the selections are complete, the builder brings the configuration together in a build summary. The updated total gives visitors a clear indication of the price before proceeding to the cart.

The final summary brings the selected options and updated price together before adding the custom knife to the cart.
